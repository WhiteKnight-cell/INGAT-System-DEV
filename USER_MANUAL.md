# USER_MANUAL.md — Community Member Guide (Sprint 8)

## What is INGAT?
INGAT helps you report environmental violations in a simple way—even if you don’t know the complicated formal process. You can submit a complaint using clear, everyday language.

You can also:
- Generate a formal letter addressed to the right government agency
- Track the status of your complaint

---

## 1) Create an Account
1. Open: **/user/register**
2. Fill out:
   - Full Name
   - Email
   - Contact Number
   - Barangay
   - Municipality
   - Password + Confirm Password
3. Submit.
4. Check your email for an OTP (verification code).
5. Go to the verification page and enter the OTP.
6. After verification, log in.

---

## 2) Log In
1. Open: **/user/login**
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Login**.

---

## 3) Submit a Complaint (Step-by-Step)
Open: **/user/submit**

1. **Choose Violation Type**
   - Illegal Dumping
   - Air Pollution
   - Water Pollution
   - Illegal Logging
   - Others

2. **Enter Street Address**
   - Write a general address or landmark to help identify where the incident happened.

3. **Enter Barangay and Municipality**
   - These will be used to show a map at the broader level.

4. **Select Date of Incident**
   - Choose the date the incident occurred.

5. **Describe the Incident**
   - Write what happened using clear details.
   - Minimum description length must be met.

### Using Voice Input (New Feature)
1. Click the **Microphone** button.
2. Choose the language:
   - **English** or **Filipino**
3. Speak clearly. Your words will appear in the description field.
4. Use the buttons:
   - **Stop** — stops recording and appends the final text
   - **Re-record** — clears current speech and lets you try again

Tip: After voice transcription, you can still manually edit the text before submitting.

---

## 4) Preview and Generate the Formal Letter
After submitting, you will see a success page showing your complaint.

If a formal letter is available, you can:
- Preview it
- Download it as **PDF** or **DOCX**

If the system cannot connect to Gemini, it will still generate a letter using an offline fallback template.

---

## 5) Track Your Complaint
Open: **/user/my-reports**

You can filter by:
- Status (Submitted / Under Review / Forwarded / Resolved)
- Violation Type
- Complaint ID search

Click a report to view the details and status history.

---

## 6) Download Exports
From your complaint detail page, you can download:
- **PDF** — for printing or sharing
- **DOCX** — for editing in Microsoft Word

---

## Support Tips
- If you cannot access your complaint detail page, make sure you are logged in to the account that submitted it.
- If your email verification fails, request a resend OTP (subject to cooldown).
